Why Custom Packaging Trays Matter

Packaging that is inadequate or does not suit the product may result in damage to goods, loss of storage space, and complications in the material handling process. A custom-made tray that corresponds to the exact geometric contours of the component in question minimizes handling defects and eliminates transit vibration.

With rich experience in the industrial packaging industry, S Square Enterprises manufactures specially designed trays engineered for protecting your high-value parts — guaranteeing the best, safest, and most ergonomic fit.

Materials We Use for Reliable Protection

The choice of material determines how effective a packaging tray will be over its operational lifetime. As a leading tray manufacturer in Pimpri-Chinchwad, we utilize various materials tailored to strength and application suitability:

Polymer Substrate Performance Properties & Best Use
ABS & HIPS Provides long-lasting, high impact-resistance and structural rigidity for heavy engineering components.
PETG Optically clear, shatter-resistant material with high chemical resistance for visual inspection and medical uses.
Conductive & Anti-Static (ESD) Materials Surface resistivity from 10^4 to 10^9 ohms/sq to safely dissipate static charges away from microelectronics.
Polypropylene (PP) Durable, lightweight, chemical-resistant polymer ideal for reusable material handling loops and washing cycles.
Corrugated Plastic Sheets Cost-effective, highly durable, water-resistant flute sheets for high-volume dunnage and divider boxes.

Our staff assists clients in choosing the right material based on the intended load capacity of the trays and the frequency of reuse required.

Custom Design and Tooling Support

Every packaging tray needs to fit its product precisely, which is why customization drives our manufacturing process. Our design and tooling capabilities include:

  • Custom Mold Design: Created from 3D CAD data or 3D-scanned physical part samples for a perfect zero-rattle fit.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Quick sample development to validate physical fit and clearance before committing to production tooling.
  • Multi-Cavity Layouts: Engineered cavity spacing for high-density packing, robot gripper clearance, and automated assembly lines.
  • Stackable & Nestable Engineering: Designed with interlock ribs and 180-degree stack/nest features to optimize storage and return freight costs.
  • Flexible Production Volumes: Supporting trial prototyping batches up to large continuous production runs.

This hands-on approach allows S Square Enterprises to support projects from initial design through to full-scale production manufacturing.
