Product Overview

S-Square Enterprises designs and manufactures custom thermoformed Industrial Packaging Trays, heavy-duty component handling dunnage, anti-static ESD carrier trays, and precision protective enclosures engineered for automotive and electronics manufacturing.

Key Performance Features

  • ESD conductive & anti-static plastic trays preventing electrostatic damage in electronic assembly
  • High impact resistance protecting sensitive automotive and machined components during transit
  • Custom cavity contours and indexing pockets matching exact part 3D CAD geometries
  • Reusable, washable, and nestable/stackable design for lean manufacturing logistics
  • Thermoformed from premium virgin and recycled engineering grade polymers

Industrial Applications

EV Battery Cell & Module Inter-Plant Transport Trays
Automotive Precision Machined Part Protection Trays
Electronic PCB & Semi-Conductor Assembly ESD Trays
Automated Robotic Feeder & Conveyor Pallet Trays

Available Specifications

Tray Polymers HDPE, HIPS, ABS, Conductive ESD PET, Polypropylene (PP)
Sheet Thickness 1.0mm to 8.0mm Heavy Gauge
Surface Resistivity 10^4 to 10^9 Ohms/sq (ESD / Conductive Grades)
Color Options Black (Standard/ESD), White, Natural, Custom Tints
Reusability Heavy-Duty Multi-Trip Returnable Industrial Packaging

Benefits & Advantages

Zero part scratches, abrasions, or damage during factory transit
Streamlines assembly line automation with precise orientation indexing
Eliminates single-use packaging waste in sustainable supply chains
In-house rapid mold fabrication & production in Bhosari, Pune
