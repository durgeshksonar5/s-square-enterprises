Materials That Keep Stickers Performing Longer

The main difference between a durable sticker and one that won't adhere for long periods of time is the proper substrate. We form solid industrial stickers with reliable materials, which suit every specific case:

Substrate Material Performance & Application Purpose
Industrial Vinyl Used for high durability, flexibility, and versatility across multiple surfaces.
Polyester (PET) Chosen for its outstanding resistance to chemical agents, oils, heat, and scratching.
Paper-Based Substrate Offered for users who choose cost-effective solutions for short-term tasks.
Reflective Stock Used for high visibility and hazard warning signs in low-light environments.
Laminated Overcoat Used for prevention of UV-fading, moisture infiltration, and mechanical abrasion.

Every production batch of material is tested for thickness, adhesion, and print quality.
