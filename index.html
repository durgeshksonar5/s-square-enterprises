Our Products & Manufacturing Offerings :--

Precision Industrial Manufacturing Products

S Square Enterprises (SIPL) manufactures high-quality industrial labels, decals, battery insulations, gaskets, and vacuum forming plastic components.

Aluminium Plates & Laser Marking Labels

High-durability metal nameplates, laser marking labels, and industrial chassis tags engineered for extreme temperatures and outdoor use.

Outdoor Industrial Decals

Weatherproof, UV-resistant commercial vehicle wraps, equipment decals, and long-lasting outdoor fleet identification stickers.

Custom Industrial Stickers & Danger Warnings

High-voltage danger warnings, battery cell safety labels, and international compliance safety decals for EV and industrial power packs.

Automobile Vacuum Forming Parts

Custom vacuum forming plastic components, automotive interior garnishes, trays, and protective handling enclosures.

FR PP PVC FRPC Battery & BESS Insulation Parts

Flame-retardant PP, PVC, and FRPC insulation components engineered for electric vehicles and Battery Energy Storage Systems.

FR Grade Cell Separators & Flame Retardant Foam

Polycarbonate and polypropylene thermal cell spacers in UL94V-0 flame retardant grade with optional pressure-sensitive adhesive.

Polycarbonate Insulation & Night Glowing Signs

Phosphorescent radium night-glowing exit signs and premium polycarbonate safety decals for facility emergency pathways.

Epoxy Fiberglass Parts, Mica & Barley Paper

High-temperature mica paper insulation sheets, mica tapes, barley paper, epoxy fiberglass sheets, and die-cut ring insulators.

Adhesive Tape Die-Cuts & Conductive Tapes

Industrial adhesive tapes, precision foam die-cuts, Kapton polyimide tapes, and custom-slit conductive tapes.

Industrial Packaging Trays & Gaskets

Custom thermoformed component handling trays, silicone foam gaskets, and NBR/EPDM industrial rubber seals.
