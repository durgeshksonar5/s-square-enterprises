Product Overview

S-Square Enterprises specializes in precision manufacturing of high-performance Flame Retardant (FR) Foam Gaskets, closed-cell silicone foam seals, EPDM, Nitrile, and CR neoprene die-cut gaskets engineered for critical sealing in automotive, EV battery, and industrial enclosures.

Key Performance Features

  • UL94 V-0 flame-retardant certified silicone and elastomeric foam formulations
  • IP65, IP67, and IP68 rated dust, moisture, and water-ingress protection
  • Excellent compression set resistance providing long-lasting seal integrity
  • Available with high-bond acrylic or pressure-sensitive adhesive (PSA) backing
  • Custom CNC knife, laser cut, and rotary die-cut geometries to tight tolerances

Industrial Applications

EV Battery Pack Top Cover & Tray Sealing Gaskets
BESS & Electrical Control Panel IP67 Gaskets
Automotive HVAC & Lighting Weatherstrip Seals
Telecom Enclosures & Outdoor Inverter Dust Barriers

Available Specifications

Gasket Polymers Silicone Foam, EPDM Sponge, CR Neoprene, NBR Nitrile, PU Foam
Flame Rating UL94 V-0 / UL94 HF-1 / FAR 25.853 Certified
Thickness Range 0.8mm to 25.0mm (Custom slit & die-cut)
Operating Temperature -55°C to +200°C (Silicone) / -40°C to +125°C (EPDM)
Adhesive Backing Non-Adhesive / Single-Sided PSA / Double-Sided Tissue / Acrylic Tape

Benefits & Advantages

Premium flame retardancy prevents fire propagation in battery enclosures
Superior UV, ozone, and weathering resistance for extreme outdoor longevity
Low closure force required for hermetic and IP67 sealing
Rapid prototyping and high-precision production in Bhosari, Pune
