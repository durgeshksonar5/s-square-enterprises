Product Overview

S-Square Enterprises produces heavy-gauge and thin-gauge custom vacuum formed plastic parts using ABS, HIPS, HDPE, and Polycarbonate polymers. From automotive interior trims and dashboard garnishes to reusable industrial component handling trays.

Key Performance Features

  • Heavy-gauge thermoforming from 1.0mm up to 8.0mm sheet thickness
  • High dimensional accuracy with 5-axis CNC routing and trimming
  • Impact-resistant ABS, HIPS, Polycarbonate, and Conductive ESD materials
  • Custom textured, color-matched, or UV-stabilized plastic sheets
  • Prototyping to large volume production runs with tooling support

Industrial Applications

Automotive Interior Pillar Covers, Trims & Dash Garnishes
EV Battery Module Handling Trays & Component Transport Trays
Commercial Vehicle Wheel Arch Covers & Engine Housings
Industrial Equipment Protective Covers & Plastic Enclosures

Available Specifications

Process Vacuum Forming / Thermoforming & 5-Axis CNC Trimming
Polymer Sheets ABS, HIPS, HDPE, Polycarbonate (PC), PETG, ESD Plastic
Sheet Thickness 1.0mm to 8.0mm
Max Forming Size Custom Large Format Moulding Capabilities
Surface Finish Leather Grain, Smooth Matte, Glossy, Textured

Benefits & Advantages

Lower tooling costs compared to traditional plastic injection molding
Lightweight, durable, and impact-resistant automotive components
Reusable packaging trays protect high-value parts during factory transport
Comprehensive design-to-moulding manufacturing in Pune
