Adhesive Tape Die Cuts Manufacturer in India

S Square Enterprises is a well-established adhesive tape die cuts manufacturer in India, producing custom die-cut tape parts used for bonding, sealing, gasketing, and assembly across a wide range of industries.

Behind many of the products we use every day is a small but critical component: a precisely cut piece of adhesive tape holding everything together. From simple mounting pads to complex multi-layer shapes, our die-cutting process converts adhesive tape rolls into precise, ready-to-use components that fit directly into your production line — saving time, reducing waste, and ensuring consistent bonding performance.

Why Die-Cut Adhesive Tapes Matter in Manufacturing

Using raw tape rolls on a production line often leads to inconsistent application, wasted material, and slower assembly times. Die-cut adhesive components solve this by delivering exact shapes and sizes ready for immediate use — no measuring, trimming, or guesswork required.

As an experienced adhesive tape die cuts manufacturer in India, S Square Enterprises converts a wide range of adhesive tapes into custom parts that integrate smoothly into automated and manual assembly processes alike, improving both speed and consistency on the shop floor.

S Square Enterprises manufactures a broad variety of die-cut adhesive tape components tailored to different industrial needs:

Foam Tape Die Cuts

Customization Range: Each product can be custom die-cut in virtually any shape, size, thickness, and packaging format (individual parts, sheets, or continuous kiss-cut rolls).

Materials We Work With

The kind of performance you get with a die-cut adhesive part varies significantly depending on the base carrier and adhesive chemistry. We manufacture different kinds of die-cut adhesive tapes at our facility in India:

Substrate / Tape Chemistry Performance & Primary Function
Acrylic Foam Tape (VHB) Provides exceptional viscoelastic strength for permanent, durable structural bonding.
Polyethylene (PE) Foam Tape Ideal for cost-effective cushioning, gap filling, and protective mounting applications.
Polyurethane (PU) Foam Tape Used for flexible, highly conformable gasketing, dust sealing, and acoustic dampening.
Thermally Conductive Tapes Engineered with ceramic heat-conduction fillers to efficiently cool electronics and power packs.
High-Temperature Silicone & Rubber Withstands extreme thermal spikes, chemical exposure, and difficult low-surface-energy substrates.

We help you select the right adhesive chemistry and carrier materials according to required bonding strength, temperature conditions, and substrate surface energy.

Custom Die-Cutting and Design Capabilities

Every application has unique shape and performance requirements, which is why customization is central to our die-cutting process. Our converting capabilities include:

  • Precision Rotary & Flatbed Die-Cutting: Clean, tight tolerances for intricate and complex 2D shapes.
  • Multi-Layer Lamination: Combining foam carriers, barrier films, and specialized liner systems.
  • Kiss-Cutting & Extended Pull Tabs: Packaged on continuous rolls with extended liners for fast manual peel-and-stick or automated pick-and-place assembly.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Quick sample turnaround for product development, design validation, and fit testing.
  • Flexible Order Volumes: Accommodating initial trial batches up to multi-million part production runs.

This adaptability allows S Square Enterprises to support projects ranging from quick prototype validation to large-scale mass manufacturing orders.
