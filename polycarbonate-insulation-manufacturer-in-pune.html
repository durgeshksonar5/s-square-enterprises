Safety with electricity as well as structural strength in every construction depend on only one factor: the right insulating material.

Due to its high dielectric strength, impact-resistance, and thermal stability, polycarbonate has proved to be one of the most desired materials when both safety and durability should be combined. Why Polycarbonate Is a Preferred Insulation Material

Polycarbonate is unique among insulators because it does not require compromising between electrical properties and mechanical properties. Polycarbonate insulation exhibits excellent dielectric characteristics, preventing electrical defects and offering resistance to impact, cracking, and distortion far better than other plastics.

S Square Enterprises, the leading polycarbonate insulation producer in India, leverages the advantages of polycarbonate insulation to create insulation components for electrical busbars, battery boxes, and industrial equipment that function flawlessly in demanding conditions.

Material Properties That Matter

The utility characteristics of polycarbonate make it the right choice for insulation applications where different demanding requirements overlap. Being a known manufacturer of specialized polycarbonate insulation in India, we use certain grades of this thermoplastic polymer that comply with:

Key Polycarbonate Property Functional Advantage in Industrial Applications
High Electrical & Dielectric Strength Prevents high-voltage breakdown, electrical arcing, and short circuits in compact designs.
Outstanding Impact Resistance Virtually unbreakable, shatter-proof structure providing robust physical and mechanical protection.
Thermal Stability Across Wide Temperatures Maintains mechanical rigidity and dielectric performance across broad operating ranges (-40°C to +130°C).
Flame-Retardant (FRPC) Formulations Meets UL94 V-0 and VTM-0 flammability standards for critical electrical and automotive safety.
UV-Stabilized & Weather-Resistant Grade Prevents yellowing, surface embrittlement, and optical degradation under outdoor solar exposure.

We assist our customers in selecting the appropriate polycarbonate grade according to requirements related to voltage rating, environmental exposure, and mechanical loading.
