What Is Vacuum Forming and Why It Matters

In the vacuum forming process, an acrylic or thermoplastic sheet is heated, placed over the mold, and formed with the use of vacuum pressure. Because it is an economical method of making certain plastic items, especially those with large to medium-sized dimensions, vacuum forming is a popular alternative to injection molding, where tooling and equipment costs can be excessive.

S Square Enterprises is a vacuum forming parts manufacturer that uses the vacuum forming process to produce high-quality parts, characterized by very strict tolerances, fine surface quality, and ability to produce multiple batches efficiently, regardless whether a client needs a one-off prototype or a large production batch.

Materials We Work With

The choice of plastic sheet material is essential to the functionality of a vacuum-formed part in practical applications. As an expert in vacuum forming products, we utilize many engineered materials, such as:

Material Grade Key Properties & Application Purpose
ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) Provides exceptional strength, structural rigidity, and high impact resistance for heavy-duty applications.
HIPS (High Impact Polystyrene) Ideal for low-cost, general-purpose thermoformed components, packaging trays, and point-of-sale displays.
PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol) Offers optical clarity, chemical resistance, toughness, and suitability for food & medical packaging applications.
Polycarbonate (PC) Delivers ultra-high strength, impact resistance, and heat-resistant performance in demanding environments.
PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) Used for outdoor components or industrial operations exposed to harsh weather, flames, and corrosive agents.

Our engineering experts assist clients in making informed decisions, choosing the right material depending on loading needs, environmental exposure, and budget limitations.
