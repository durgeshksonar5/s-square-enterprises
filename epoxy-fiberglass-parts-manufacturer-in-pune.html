Why Epoxy Fiberglass Is Valued Across Industries

Epoxy fiberglass sheets have been designed to handle mechanical stresses, have resistance to moisture absorption, and they are able to provide insulation against electricity even when constant load or high temperature is applied to this material. Unlike many plastic materials which can be used alone, this composite material preserves its properties and offers strength throughout its lifetime.

As a company specialized in the manufacturing of epoxy fiberglass parts in India, S Square Enterprises is able to produce components taking great advantage of these properties and using them successfully in electrical equipment, machines, and construction.

Material Grades and Performance Properties

Choosing the appropriate epoxy fiberglass grade is critical in ensuring that the performance of the material is in line with the specific application requirements. Being a renowned manufacturer of epoxy fiberglass components in India, we emphasize laminate grades characterized by:

Key Material Property Performance Advantage in Demanding Environments
Significant Mechanical Strength High tensile, flexural, and compressive modulus retaining rigidity even under heavy mechanical load.
Excellent Dielectric Properties High breakdown voltage and low dissipation factor leading to effective, reliable electrical insulation.
Minimal Moisture Absorption Extremely low water absorption rate (<0.1%) which fosters outstanding dimensional stability over time.
Broad Heat Resistance Range Thermal endurance across Class B (130°C), Class F (155°C), and Class H (180°C) operating standards.
Chemical & Solvent Resistance Resistant to transformer oils, corrosive industrial chemicals, acids, and industrial atmospheres.

Our engineers assist clients in determining the right laminate grade (FR-4, G-10, G-11) depending on voltage level, mechanical stress, and operational environmental conditions.
