What Makes Industrial Decals Different from Standard Stickers?

Not all adhesive graphics are made for industrial applications. Regular stickers can dissolve, break, or come off when used in oil, UV light or high temperatures, which are very common on factory floors or outdoor equipment. S Square Enterprises is a renowned producer of industrial stickers in India and makes their stickers from advanced materials and raw materials that resist well to such threats.

Before being shipped, each of our stickers is tested under pressure to find out how strong they stick and whether they are UV resistant, chemically resistant, and resistant to heat.

Materials Built for Industrial Durability

The choice of material decides the performance of a decal, and thus, we at a specialist manufacturer of industrial decals in India carefully select the substrate:

Substrate Material Performance & Durability Advantage
Cast & Calendered Vinyl High flexibility, conformability to curved surfaces, and outstanding outdoor weather protection.
Polyester (PET) Films High tensile strength, dimensional stability, and extreme chemical and abrasion resistance.
Reflective Sheeting (Prismatic / Beaded) Superior retroreflection for night-time and low-light visibility on industrial vehicles and hazards.
Polycarbonate & Metal Foils Stiff permanence, impact resistance, and subsurface graphic protection for harsh machinery wear.
Anti-UV Overlaminate Protective clear over-lamination to avoid fading, yellowing, scratching, and cracking under direct sunlight.

Before production, each class of material goes through a check for thickness, sharpness of the print, and adhesive quality.
