Why Flame Retardant Foam Matters

Businesses often use standard foam materials that ignite quickly, causing fire to spread faster because of the heat and smoke generated by them. This can lead to disastrous consequences, especially if the foam material used is placed inside a closed area like a car or cupboard of appliance working on battery.

FR foam (Flame Retardant foam) has chemical additives that are used to increase their fire-resistance, allowing them to suppress the flames themselves and produce fewer harmful emissions when they ignite.

As a trustworthy producer of flame-retardant foam, S Square Enterprises manufactures multiple kinds of foam offering a good combination of safety features and usability to its customers.

Materials We Use for Fire-Safe Performance

Choosing the right foam formula is important for balancing the needs for fire safety and mechanical properties. As a manufacturer of fireproof foam products, we apply different fireproof materials:

Foam Substrate Functional Performance & Safety Profile
Fireproof Polyurethane (PU) Foam Well-known for its exceptional performance in shock absorption, mechanical cushioning, and dust sealing.
Melamine Foam Ultralight acoustic absorber and high-temperature thermal insulator with natural flame retardancy.
FR EPDM Foam Closed-cell elastomer highly resistant to chemicals, oils, ozone, UV exposure, and outdoor weather.
Silicone Foam Withstands extreme temperatures (-60°C to +200°C), retains elasticity, and offers low compression set.
Cross-Linked PE (XLPE) Foam Effective for long-term resistance to physical deformation, moisture penetration, and thermal bridging.

Our technical specialists help clients find the right formula in accordance with the operating temperature, mechanical load, and UL94 fire safety standards.
